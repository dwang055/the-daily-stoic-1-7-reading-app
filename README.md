## What you can do

| Feature | How to use it |
|---|---|
| **Listen and read along** | Press ▶. Each sentence and word lights up as it is read. Tap any sentence to hear it; 🔁 repeats it; choose a slower speed. |
| **Learn new words** | Tap a gold-underlined word for its pronunciation, Chinese meaning, simple English and 🔊. |
| **Read in Chinese too** | **译** shows the Chinese translation under each paragraph. |
| **Word list** | **⭐ 生词 Words**: every new word with sound. Mark the ones you know with **熟词**. |
| **Practise** | **✏️ 练习 Practice**: flashcards, matching, fill the gap, and listen & choose. |
| **Make it comfortable** | **Aa** changes the text size; ☀️ / 🌙 switches light and dark. |
